OUR PURPOSE

Make every dealership decision more informed.

Cars Dealership is a full-stack capstone application that brings dealership details and customer reviews together in one clear, easy-to-use experience.

What we do

Visitors can explore dealership listings, filter by state, open a dealer profile, and read customer feedback. Registered users can share their own purchase experiences.

Explore

Review dealership locations and contact information.

Compare

Read feedback before deciding which dealer to visit.

Contribute

Share a review with purchase and vehicle details.
